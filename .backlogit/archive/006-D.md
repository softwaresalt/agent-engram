# Markdown compaction and historical condensation investigation

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
We need a bounded investigation into whether compacted derivative markdown can improve token efficiency and retrieval quality without destructively rewriting canonical historical records.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
- Leave historical markdown unchanged and rely only on retrieval ranking\n- Generate compacted derivative artifacts while preserving canonical originals\n- Allow destructive rewriting of historical docs
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Investigate derivative-only compaction. Preserve canonical source material and evaluate compacted representations centered on decisions, plans, objectives, constraints, open questions, and outcomes.
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->

## Open Questions

<!-- BEGIN:open-questions -->
- Which document classes are safe to compact versus too risky?\n- What retrieval gains are realistic?\n- Can smaller models assist safely?
<!-- END:open-questions -->

## Notes
