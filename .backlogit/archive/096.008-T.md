F7 recall parity (recall-safe subset, Anchor B): document the release-gate check that every unique cross-file bare call resolving via the legacy name-only matcher before the feature AND not intentionally suppressed by a typed fail-closed reason still resolves after it (Y3/F3 fallback for {NoModuleContext, UnsupportedImportForm}), so python_bare routing introduces no recall regression on that subset; intentional fail-closed drops ({CompetingBindings, Shadowed, DuplicateSameNameImport} -- no edge, Anchor B) are EXPECTED, not regressions.
