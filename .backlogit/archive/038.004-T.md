Add async retry logic with exponential backoff around CozoDB write batches in
`index_workspace` so concurrent workspaces don't panic on SQLITE_BUSY during
write transactions.

Fix: `run_script_busy_retry_mutable` private method on `CodeGraphQueries`; all
nine `run_script(Mutable)` calls in `upsert_function`, `upsert_class`,
`upsert_interface` retry independently. Top-level `run_with_busy_retry` wrapper
removed (was unsafe: content_hash committed before symbol writes, causing
mid-file retry to skip the file).

- `s_cs4` concurrent indexing test un-ignored and passes
- No regressions in sequential indexing paths
