Migrate Startup plus legacy/v2 watcher paths to guarded admission and parent-retained DriverTaskGuard supervision. No permit-associated cancellation receiver or task handle may detach.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
1. All 109.024-T scenarios pass; Startup/both Watchers create guarded Waiting registrations before recheck, drop those registrations on acquisition, move cancellation/binding ownership into exact permits, and preserve behavior.
2. Outer daemon scope retains both watcher task guards; normal shutdown consumes/joins, and parent cancellation/drop aborts. Each spawned future holds its permit outside an inner mutation-capable iteration so child work drops before permit terminal.
3. ?, snapshot failure, early return, cancellation, panic, task abort, and handle loss cannot strand ownership/full mask. No successor runs before quiescent ack; max one DB driver and zero post-ack old work.
4. Ordinary completion disarms; Released wakes after unlock; cancellation Drop recovers Running or acknowledges Retiring after unwind. Stale terminals cannot mutate replacement state.
5. Remove try-then-set and finish-then-drain. No raw detached mutator, forced barrier clear, mutex across await, public seam, receiver getter/clone, unsafe, or wire/schema/response change.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->
