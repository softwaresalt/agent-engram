title: Engram daemon reliability program
updated_at: 2026-04-21T09:40:24.5963591-07:00
---

Eliminate the class of perceived-unreliability issues where users (and the developer) hit "engram won't load" or "engram is confused after /new" and have to manually clean up `.engram/run/` state. Issues are caused by stale runtime state, version drift between shim and daemon binaries, silent registry-source mismatches, workspace-hash drift across sessions, and lack of operator-visible health diagnostics.

Goal: make engram self-healing and self-diagnosing so the failure modes documented in this feature either cannot happen or surface a clear, actionable error before agents make tool calls.
