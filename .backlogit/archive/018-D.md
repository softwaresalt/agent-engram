# Shared HCL parser for Terraform-family files

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
Index .hcl, .tf, and .tfvars through one safe, composable HCL parser service. Preserve workspace containment, fail-closed indexing, tree-sitter 0.25 compatibility, test-first delivery, and local-daemon operational reliability.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
A. Duplicate Terraform parsers by extension: rejected for drift and complexity. B. One canonical Language::Hcl parser with all three extensions: preferred for cohesion and interoperability. C. Defer behind another compatibility spike: unnecessary after read-only inspection of the exact published crate proved a tree-sitter-language 0.1 bridge and tree-sitter 0.25.3 development contract.
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Proceed with one Language::Hcl service shared by .hcl/.tf/.tfvars, exact-pin tree-sitter-hcl 1.1.0, map all extensions to canonical hcl, make HCL available in default code-graph languages, extract top-level block/attribute symbols and expression traversals as references, and wire startup traversal plus live-sync routing. Deliver contract/unit/integration harnesses before production units. No workspace Rust unsafe blocks.
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->

## Open Questions

<!-- BEGIN:open-questions -->
Runtime ABI/load behavior, exact AST traversal semantics, and daemon indexing must be proven by Ship in RED/GREEN tests; Stage will not execute the untrusted grammar or cargo. The crates.io archive is compatible by manifest/binding inspection but its v1.1.0 Git tag does not reproduce the published Rust binding, so pin/checksum and provenance review are mandatory.
<!-- END:open-questions -->
