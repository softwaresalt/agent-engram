## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
PR #302 review identified two medium-priority, non-P0/P1 residual races in the same daemon pending-sync state machine after archived 105-F. FF55E51A covers (T1) the new workspace/config binding becoming visible before sync_generation advances and (T3) publish_pending_sync loading generation outside the pending_sync mutex, allowing stale-generation loss or companion-bit leakage. 88EB5FB1 covers the sibling startup producer retaining the pre-R2 failed-try then set_pending_sync pattern, so a startup intent can land after a holder's final empty-queue peek without a guaranteed finisher. Low stash 1E70A289 corrects the same state.rs generation-lock and lost-wakeup proof comments and is superseded by the FF code change. The unit must remain daemon lifecycle/pending-sync width only; no Python, Spark, SQL, PowerBI, Cozo, schema, CLI response, or 015-D IPC-hang work.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Choose A. Create one new medium-priority feature related to archived 105-F, with four dependency-ordered, single-width tasks: deterministic RED generation-transition harness; bounded GREEN generation/binding/publish linearization plus same-file proof comments; deterministic RED startup final-peek harness; bounded GREEN startup producer backstop and guaranteed finisher. Each task is capped below two hours, fewer than three files, fewer than five functions, and at most three scenarios. No public API/schema/migration/CLI response change. 1E70A289 is folded because both requested comment corrections are in the exact state.rs primitive changed by FF55E51A; it adds no independent width. 5765BAAB/015-D and every unrelated stash entry remain deferred. Source decision: docs/decisions/2026-07-31-post-105-pending-sync-residuals-deliberation.md. Planned implementation: docs/exec-plans/2026-07-31-post-105-pending-sync-residuals-plan.md.
