Implement atomic binding advance with one fail-closed quiescence barrier and complete old-generation signaling. Prepare/capacity-check the tuple, acquire binding guards in fixed order, then perform one no-await coordinator transition.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
1. All 109.016-T scenarios pass; overflow failures mutate nothing and new admission is minted only after coherent publication.
2. Publication replaces the channel, unlocks, then signals the old channel. This reaches idle Waiting admission guards and every active OwnerPermit even when no owner transition or coordinator notification follows.
3. A running owner becomes Retiring, not Idle: same binding moves owner mask OR pending into deferred; distinct binding moves zero old bits. Current requests only coalesce/wait and no successor acquires before exact ack.
4. Exact terminal/Drop after DB/file-capable exit publishes latest deferred, clears the barrier, writes no timestamp, and wakes once. Rebind while retiring retargets one barrier; returning to the retired binding restores its immutable owner|pending replay, intermediary-target work stays discarded, and stale terminals do nothing.
5. Direct kind and move-only cancellation/binding ownership from 109.015-T remain intact; acquisition retains no waiter registration and no receiver is extracted or detached from Waiting/OwnerPermit.
6. No mutex crosses await and no public/wire/schema/config-format/persistence change occurs.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->
