# Retain one .engram authority through daemon-key selection

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
daemon_key_for_workspace resolves one GitMetadata root but independently opens .engram in the presence probe, workspace-id load/publish, and PID fallback. A rename/substitution between opens can mix child-directory objects under one root proof.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
A) revalidate .engram after every read; B) open .engram once and pass the retained CapRoot through probe, UUID read/publish, and PID read; C) merge with lifecycle bind composition.
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Choose B as a separate prerequisite release unit. Revalidation does not close substitution windows; combining with lifecycle composition exceeds width and obscures RED/GREEN boundaries. 1CB366DB must follow this unit.
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->

## Open Questions

<!-- BEGIN:open-questions -->
Exact API ownership shape must preserve the cold-start case where .engram is absent and the legacy-live-PID branch where it exists. Multi-model adversarial review remains a harvest blocker.
<!-- END:open-questions -->
