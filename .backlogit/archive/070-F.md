Fix the two incrementally-fixable correctness bugs the 069-S evaluation gate surfaced in the safe TMDL line/indent parser (`crates/powerbi-tmdl-parser/src/lib.rs`, adapted by `src/services/powerbi_tmdl.rs`), as recorded in the eval finding `docs/decisions/2026-07-04-tmdl-eval-gate-finding.md` and already pinned (asserting the buggy behavior) by the differential harness `tests/unit/tmdl_differential_eval_test.rs`. This feature implements exactly the "optional, bounded follow-on" the DECLINE finding itself proposed — no grammar, no new dependency, crate stays `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`. Two test-first tasks: calculated-column expression capture (070.001-T, flips S-PTM-24 + S-PTM-28) and the measure-DAX colon heuristic (070.002-T, flips S-PTM-25). The four model-richness gaps (S-PTM-21 relationship qualifiers, S-PTM-23 hierarchy/level, S-PTM-26 calculationGroup, S-PTM-27 RLS role) are explicitly OUT of scope — candidate future work, not this shipment. Plan: docs/exec-plans/2026-07-04-tmdl-parser-correctness-fixes-plan.md.

<!-- BEGIN:goals -->
- Fix calculated-column mis-scoping: for `column X = <DAX>`, split the declaration name on the first top-level `=` (mirror `parse_measure_declaration` at lib.rs:920) and capture the DAX into a new additive `TmdlColumn.expression: Option<String>`, including the multiline `column X =` / next-line-body form (mirror the `PendingMeasureBody` capture machinery). Column name and expression become distinct on the produced `TmdlModel` (070.001-T).
- Fix measure-DAX `:` truncation: refine `looks_like_tmdl_property` (lib.rs:1128, currently a bare `trimmed.contains(':')`) so a measure/DAX body line such as `FORMAT ( NOW (), "HH:mm:ss" )` is not misclassified as a TMDL property boundary — require a property-shaped `key:` (leading bare identifier immediately followed by `:`) rather than the mere presence of a colon (070.002-T).
- Preserve the safe, dependency-light parser boundary: additive, back-compat surfaces only; crate stays `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`; no new dependency; no grammar/tree-sitter work; no schema/indexer/CLI change.
