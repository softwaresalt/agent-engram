# Sequence workspace authority retention follow-ups

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
PR #353 retained CapRoot authority inside workspace resolution, but bind composition and daemon-key child-directory composition remain separate TOCTOU surfaces. Decide whether to combine them or serialize width-isolated release units.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
A) one feature/shipment spanning src/db/workspace.rs and src/tools/lifecycle.rs; B) separate ordered units with 7B15B447 first and 1CB366DB second; C) independent unordered units.
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Choose B. Harden the internal daemon-key .engram authority first, then make the lifecycle bind consume one combined canonical-path/UUID/branch proof. This minimizes width, preserves independent RED/GREEN edges, and prevents the combined bind API from depending on an identity path that still reopens .engram.
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->
