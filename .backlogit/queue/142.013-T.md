Plan unit F08 - atomic publication under a publisher lock. Implement `src/services/generations/publish.rs` using the exact safe-Rust atomic replacement primitive selected by the F01 spike. Publication takes a cross-process publisher lock, enforces the checked revision guard, performs a durable replacement of the active-generation manifest, and ignores - never promotes - orphaned temporary files.

The durable manifest is the sole publication authority. There is no notification, no control endpoint, and no reload signal: activation is triggered only by the daemon reconciling this manifest at startup and at request entry.

Execution posture: test-first concurrency boundary. RS4 - this unit is ActionRisk high and requires operator approval before Ship implements it.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
- Publication acquires a cross-process publisher lock; two concurrent publishers serialize rather than interleave.
- The checked revision guard refuses a non-increasing revision.
- Replacement uses the F01-selected primitive and is durable (create-and-rename in the same directory, with the required fsync discipline).
- The manifest includes generation identity and schema version.
- Orphaned temporary files are ignored and never promoted to active.
- A crash at any point leaves either the prior manifest or the new manifest intact - never a torn or partial manifest.
- No notification, reload signal, or generation-control endpoint is introduced.
- `tests/integration/generation_publish_test.rs` covers concurrency and crash recovery.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->
