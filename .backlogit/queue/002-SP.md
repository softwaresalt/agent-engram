INDEPENDENT defect, deliberately EXCLUDED from the server/discover compatibility shipment to preserve width isolation. Evidence from the 2026-08-23 spike: the 135 MB Cozo database took roughly 7.5 minutes to become ready (daemon started 16:35:42, database quiesced 16:42:44, session indexing recorded 16:43:12), consumed about 1.37 GB, and accumulated about 893 CPU-seconds while reporting 'starting' beyond repeated 30-second readiness windows. Goal: identify which Cozo open or schema-bootstrap operation dominates the cold start. Constraint: increasing the shim/daemon readiness timeout is NOT an acceptable outcome - it masks the symptom without fixing the expensive open/bootstrap path. Suggested entry point: src/db/cozo_backend/mod.rs.

EXECUTED 2026-09-10. Status done. Findings artifact: docs/decisions/2026-09-10-engram-cold-start-readiness-spike.md (conclusion: proceed, confidence: medium). Option analysis and accepted direction: docs/decisions/2026-09-10-content-addressed-generation-cold-start-decision.md (Option 4 - shared immutable content-addressed generations + branch overlay). Implementation plan: docs/exec-plans/2026-09-10-cold-start-readiness-reliability-plan.md. Promoted to two release units: 143-F (Workstream A, daemon liveness safety) via shipment 143-S, and 144-F (Workstream B, cold-start observability + content-addressed generation reuse) via shipment 144-S. Bounded conclusion: every connect_db sub-phase except schema_bootstrap is hard-bounded (file_lock 30s deadline; database_open MAX_REOPEN_ATTEMPTS=10 with 250ms-capped backoff = ~1.6-2.4s), so CONDITIONAL ON THE STALL BEING PRE-READINESS, schema_bootstrap accounts for >=99.6% of pre-readiness time. That conditional qualifier is load-bearing: the spike could NOT exclude a ready-but-unreachable daemon, and 144.001-T exists in part to discriminate between the two. The dominant sub-operation within schema_bootstrap remains UNPROVEN (HNSW m:50 dim:384 x3 is the highest-support hypothesis only); 144.002-T is the measurement gate that resolves it. Raising ENGRAM_READY_TIMEOUT_MS remains REJECTED. NOT archived: retained in queue as the traceability anchor for 143-F/144-F until both shipments close.
