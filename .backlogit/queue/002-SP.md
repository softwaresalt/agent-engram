INDEPENDENT defect, deliberately EXCLUDED from the server/discover compatibility shipment to preserve width isolation. Evidence from the 2026-08-23 spike: the 135 MB Cozo database took roughly 7.5 minutes to become ready (daemon started 16:35:42, database quiesced 16:42:44, session indexing recorded 16:43:12), consumed about 1.37 GB, and accumulated about 893 CPU-seconds while reporting 'starting' beyond repeated 30-second readiness windows. Goal: identify which Cozo open or schema-bootstrap operation dominates the cold start. Constraint: increasing the shim/daemon readiness timeout is NOT an acceptable outcome - it masks the symptom without fixing the expensive open/bootstrap path. Suggested entry point: src/db/cozo_backend/mod.rs.
