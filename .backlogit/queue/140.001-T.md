- Decide whether engram adopts the v1.1.0 thin-shim contract or permanently pins its own hardened launchers.

<!-- BEGIN:description -->
The autoharness v1.5.0 merge-install rewrote `start.ps1` and `start.sh` to the generic v1.1.0 thin-shim startup-script contract. That discarded engram-specific hardening asserted by `tests/contract/start_launcher_test.rs`:

- `$process.WaitForExit($cleanupTimeoutMs)` bounded cleanup, with no unbounded `$process.WaitForExit()`
- a single shared wall-clock pre-warm budget honouring `ENGRAM_PREWARM_TIMEOUT_MS` (observed 34.4s against an 8s ceiling after the overwrite)
- no termination of unowned descendant processes

Both launchers were reverted to the `main` versions in PR #371. `autoharness verify-workspace` now reports two `degrading` migration proposals and independently marks `start.ps1` as `manual_review: true` with the action "Do not auto-apply. Surface the script for operator review", so the revert matches autoharness's own guidance. The workspace still reports 0 blockers, 0 warnings, and 68/68 targeted checks.

`start.sh` is classified `known-legacy` with a preserved 790-byte custom-section tail; `start.ps1` is `ambiguous` because its core content carries unrecognised edits.
