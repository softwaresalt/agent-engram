Migrate Hydration to guarded Waiting/OwnerPermit admission and a parent-retained spawned-task guard. Pre-acquisition and post-acquisition ownership must be continuous and cleanup non-optional.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
1. All 109.018-T scenarios pass; Waiting owns the pre-enabled registration and receiver, re-arms before recheck, and holds no standard mutex across await.
2. Zero DB connect/query/hydration/file/progress boundary occurs before Acquired(Hydration). Admission ownership moves into the permit and the receiver cannot be extracted.
3. Define the private reusable DriverTaskGuard in state.rs and make set_workspace store it instead of dropping the hydration JoinHandle. Normal completion/shutdown consumes and joins; replacement or parent cancellation Drop aborts; the mutation-capable inner future drops/joins before permit Drop or retirement ack.
4. Pre-acquisition cancellation mutates nothing. Post-acquisition cancellation, DB failure, ?, early return, panic, or abort cannot detach the driver or strand ownership; no successor runs before exact quiescent ack.
5. Ordinary handled exits complete/disarm; no-pending Released wakes after unlock. No detached child, forced timeout, second queue, public seam, caller-optional cleanup, or unsafe.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

src/server/state.rs and src/tools/lifecycle.rs only; <=4 production functions, <=3 inherited scenarios, <=110 minutes. DriverTaskGuard Drop may abort but never awaits; barrier release remains permit-terminal authority. Later write and IPC tasks reuse this guard without widening this task.
