Make Hydration register notification, request an exact cancellation-bearing permit, and wait without a standard mutex. Pre-acquisition generation cancellation exits without acknowledgment. After acquisition, Hydration observes permit cancellation at safe boundaries, drops or joins all DB/file-capable work, then explicitly acknowledges retirement or lets armed Drop acknowledge after unwind. Normal and handled failure explicitly complete/disarm.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
1. All 109.018-T scenarios pass; Notified is registered before request recheck and waits hold no standard mutex.
2. Zero DB connect/query/hydration/file/progress boundary occurs before permit acquisition.
3. Pre-acquisition cancellation mutates nothing. Post-acquisition rebind cancellation is observed and no successor runs until Hydration quiesces and acknowledges exactly once.
4. Normal/DB-failure completion disarms cleanup; retired acknowledgment writes no timestamp, wakes once, and permits no old work afterward.
5. No detached DB/file/workspace mutator, forced barrier timeout, second queue, public seam, or caller-optional cleanup.
