Make Hydration enable notification before every final request/recheck, request an exact cancellation-bearing permit, and wait without a standard mutex. Queued awaits the existing registration; every wake enables a fresh one before recheck. Pre-acquisition generation cancellation exits without acknowledgment. After acquisition, Hydration quiesces DB/file work before retirement acknowledgment or armed Drop. Normal and handled failure explicitly complete/disarm; no-pending Released passes the empty-waiter baton.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
1. All 109.018-T scenarios pass; Notified is created and enabled before every final request/recheck, Queued awaits it, and waits hold no standard mutex.
2. Zero DB connect/query/hydration/file/progress boundary occurs before permit acquisition.
3. Owner-success/no-pending completion invokes one post-unlock notification; one or multiple empty waiters progress one acquisition/release at a time without polling, notify-as-authority, or concurrent owners.
4. Pre-acquisition cancellation mutates nothing. Post-acquisition rebind cancellation is observed and no successor runs until Hydration quiesces and acknowledges exactly once; retired ack writes no timestamp and invokes one notification.
5. No detached DB/file/workspace mutator, forced barrier timeout, second queue, public seam, or caller-optional cleanup.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->
