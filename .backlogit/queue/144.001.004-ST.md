SINGLE-ATTEMPT STARTUP CLASSIFICATION, CORRELATED AND CLIENT-VISIBLE. This is a first-class requirement (R6), not a side effect. The spike could NOT exclude that the incident daemon was READY-BUT-UNREACHABLE with its CPU burn coming from post-readiness hydration - and the two conditions demand completely different fixes.

AC1 (ONE ATTEMPT, NEVER A MIXED TAIL): classification is computed over the records of a SINGLE startup-attempt ID, never over a mixed tail of the sink. A sink containing a second interleaved attempt does not change the result; asserted by test.
AC2 (CORRELATION RULE, THREE OUTCOMES, NON-NEGOTIABLE): (i) last durable record is a PRE-READINESS phase or heartbeat AND no readiness_published record exists for that attempt => GENUINE PRE-READY STALL; (ii) readiness_published exists for that attempt AND there is correlated evidence of a FAILED transport bind/registration or a FAILED/TIMED-OUT probe against THAT ATTEMPT'S endpoint => READY-BUT-UNREACHABLE; (iii) readiness_published exists AND probe/transport evidence is SUCCESSFUL or ABSENT-WITHOUT-FAILURE => HEALTHY (or post_ready_work_in_progress), explicitly NOT ready-but-unreachable.
AC3 (ALL THREE TESTED): three integration tests induce a pre-ready stall, a ready-but-unreachable daemon, and a HEALTHY start, and assert three DIFFERENT classifications. A healthy start is never classified ready-but-unreachable; the correlation requirement is asserted directly rather than inferred.
AC4 (SHARED STATUS TYPES, CLI AND MCP, CONTRACT TESTED): the classification is exposed through the SHARED HealthReport and DaemonStatus types and returned IDENTICALLY in structured status/error data through both the CLI and the MCP surface, asserted by a contract test. Operators and agents diagnosing a stall MUST NOT be required to read a JSONL file.
AC5 (PARITY DOC IN THE SAME CHANGE): docs/cli-mcp-parity.md is updated in this same change and the drift guard passes.

MEASUREMENT ONLY: this leaf classifies and reports. It changes no startup ordering and takes no corrective action.
