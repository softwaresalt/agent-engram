FIRST HALF OF THE WATCHDOG HARD DEADLINE: stop accepting, flush durably, drain cooperatively. Split from the Revision 2 single leaf in Revision 3 because that leaf carried five distinct test scenarios and exceeded the 2-hour rule. The exact-process forced exit and the reacquisition proof are owned by 143.003.006-ST.

Termination is NOT a bare abort(). A watchdog exit that leaves a held fd_lock converts a slow-start bug into a PERMANENT-WEDGE bug, strictly worse than the defect being fixed - which is why each step is separately bounded.

AC1 (HARD DEADLINE SEQUENCE, ORDERED, STEPS 1-3): on expiry the daemon MUST (1) STOP ACCEPTING new connections, (2) write and BOUNDED-FLUSH a durable terminal record to the A-owned sink (143.004.001-ST), then (3) attempt a BOUNDED COOPERATIVE DRAIN of in-flight work. Step (4), forced exit, is owned by 143.003.006-ST and is invoked by this leaf when the drain bound expires.
AC2 (EVERY STEP SEPARATELY BOUNDED, AND THE SUM IS BOUNDED): steps (2) and (3) each carry their OWN EXPLICIT BOUND from a named constant, and the SUM of the bounds is itself bounded. A test asserts that when both (2) and (3) hit their bounds, total elapsed time from expiry to the forced-exit handoff is within that sum. A stuck sub-step MUST NOT be able to extend the hard deadline indefinitely.
AC3 (DURABLE RECORD PRECEDES ANY EXIT): the terminal cause is flushed to the A-owned sink BEFORE the drain and before any exit path, since a forced exit runs no further code. A test kills the process immediately after the flush and asserts the record is present, complete, and parseable.
AC4 (ORDERLY PATH WHEN DRAIN SUCCEEDS): if in-flight work unwinds within the cooperative grace period, the ORDERLY shutdown path is taken and forced exit is NOT invoked. A test covers both branches of this decision.
AC5 (STOP-ACCEPTING IS OBSERVABLE): a connection attempt made after expiry is refused rather than accepted-then-dropped, asserted by a test.
AC6 (GATE G7, JOINT): contributes to G7 jointly with 143.003.006-ST, 143.004.001-ST, and 143.004.002-ST. No single leaf closes G7 alone.
AC7 (PA-2 APPROVAL BOUNDARY, PRECISE): CP-3 operator approval MUST be recorded BEFORE THE FIRST REAL TERMINATION EXECUTION - any execution that actually terminates a live daemon process, in production, development, or test. SIMULATION-SAFE TESTS MAY RUN BEFORE APPROVAL: tests exercising this sequence against an INJECTED or SIMULATED exit/drain seam, terminating no real daemon process, are explicitly permitted pre-approval and are the intended pre-CP-3 evidence.
AC8 (NO WORKSTREAM-B DEPENDENCY): no dependency on gate G3, on 144-F, or on any 144-S item. Memory or RSS thresholds are NOT an input to watchdog behaviour.
