Plan unit PRE-3b, Slot-04b of the 142-F decomposition plan Revision 18 (`docs/exec-plans/2026-09-30-142-f-decomposition-plan.md`, section 4.3 split S-04 of `142.064-T`, created at assembly step 4 as placeholder `142.064b`; carries `142.064.002-ST` plus the gate slot from `142.064.001-ST`). The family runs `142.076-T` (placeholder `142.064a`, Slot-04a), then this task, then `142.064-T` (Slot-04c, the last slot, which closes PRE-3). Unit source: `docs/exec-plans/2026-09-26-142-f-launcher-preflight-command-plan.md`, section "PRE-3: Production read-server startup activation wiring (stash 9B7EC1E4)", subsections "Install before readiness", "Background activation" steps 1 and 2 (first bullet), "Publication-last" and "Without a generations root". Route (c). Execution posture: test-first; safety mode: careful (ordering-critical: the install must precede the Release store on `hydration_ready`).

Scope (this task's share of PRE-3):
- Gate slot in `src/server/state.rs`: one `RwLock<Option<Arc<ReadServerStartupGate>>>` field with `pub` `set_read_server_gate` / `read_server_gate` beside `set_generation_activator`.
- Install before readiness: when `generations_root` is a directory at startup (`symlink_metadata`), call the new private `install_generation_gate(&state, &layout).await` AFTER workspace publication and BEFORE `set_hydration_ready_for_generation`: `open_generation_store(&layout, false)`; `GenerationActivator::new(store, layout.runtime_root.clone(), layout.expected_identity(), layout.activation_deadline)`; `ReadServerStartupGate::new`; `socket_bound()`; install via `set_generation_activator(Some(..))` and `set_read_server_gate(Some(..))`. No manifest read, hash, or copy; never awaits activation; `_health` unchanged.
- Background driver: after readiness, `tokio::spawn` (never awaited) the new private `drive_generation_activation(state, layout, shutdown_tx.subscribe())`; backoff 100 ms doubling to a 2 s cap; no attempt and no per-tick log while nothing is published; late install ONLY when the root was ABSENT at startup (attempt-4 P2-1: a startup install failure with the root present is final for that daemon, logged once at `warn`); `run_initial_activation` only for a non-zero revision different from the last attempted one (the transient same-revision retry is Slot-04c); errors logged once per distinct revision or error kind at `warn` with no payloads; publication-last; exits on success or shutdown.
- RED: this task writes its own RED cases at its own Step 2 in `tests/integration/read_server_generation_wiring_test.rs` (the file `142.076-T` creates).

Production files edited: `src/daemon/lifecycle_policy.rs` (`install_generation_gate`, `drive_generation_activation`, and their two call sites in `run_read_server_startup`); `src/server/state.rs` (the gate slot).

Not in this task: the identity REPLACE and the family's RED-phase record (Slot-04a, `142.076-T`); the transient same-revision retry and the PRE-3 close (Slot-04c, `142.064-T`). No F54 evidence or determinism ownership in this family (plan 4.3 and 9.2); consumer-baseline gates only.
