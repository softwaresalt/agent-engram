Implement the immutable rejection cache and transient backoff. A revision rejected for a permanent reason must never be retried; a transient failure must back off rather than spin on every request.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
- A permanently rejected revision is recorded in an immutable rejection cache and never retried.
- The rejection cache distinguishes permanent from transient failure classes.
- Transient failures back off; request-entry reconciliation does not spin.
- The cache never grows without bound in a way that affects the read path.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->
