PER-HNSW-OPERATION TIMING WITH AN EXPLICIT CREATE-VS-SKIP OUTCOME. Split from 144.002-T in Revision 3.

WHY THE OUTCOME IS LOAD-BEARING: the HNSW create path currently SUPPRESSES the "already exists" condition. With the outcome suppressed, a near-zero HNSW timing is INDISTINGUISHABLE between "cheap to build" and "skipped entirely" - so a measurement without the outcome cannot attribute build cost at all. Revision 2 tried to resolve this by depending on an implementation leaf (144.003.001-ST) that also changed behaviour; Revision 3 keeps the create-vs-skip OUTCOME RECORD inside the measurement workstream and leaves all behaviour change to the future implementation plan.

AC1 (PER-OPERATION TIMING): EACH of the three HNSW index operations (m:50, dim:384, Cosine) is timed and recorded SEPARATELY by index name.
AC2 (EXPLICIT CREATE-VS-SKIP OUTCOME PER OPERATION): each HNSW record carries an EXPLICIT outcome of exactly created or skipped_existing. A test asserts that a SECOND bootstrap against the same database reports skipped_existing for all three, and a FIRST bootstrap against a fresh database reports created for all three.
AC3 (NO SUPPRESSED-OUTCOME AMBIGUITY): a near-zero timing is never ambiguous, because the outcome field is always present. A test asserts the outcome field is populated on every HNSW record, including on the suppressed-condition path.
AC4 (MEASUREMENT ONLY, NON-NEGOTIABLE): recording the outcome MUST NOT change control flow, MUST NOT convert the suppressed condition into an error, MUST NOT move index construction off the readiness path, and MUST NOT introduce a warming state. Any such change belongs to the future implementation plan, not to 144-S. A recorded audit confirms behaviour is unchanged.
