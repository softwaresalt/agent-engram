Scope (this task's share of F50), in `crates/engram-indexer/src/preflight.rs`:
- The typed stage enum and transition table for Build -> Seal -> Publish -> DaemonVerified -> HealthVerified -> CliProbeVerified -> McpProbeVerified -> Succeeded, or a typed `Failed` naming the stage. Transitions are forward only; skipping a stage is unrepresentable.
- ONE deadline and ONE expected generation carried by the machine, not by individual stages.
- Fail-closed semantics: any failure yields `Failed`, never a degraded `Succeeded`. This supersedes the 118-S Guardrail 4 fail-open behavior: an agent session must not be able to begin with Engram degraded.
- Mock-driven cases in `tests/integration/preflight_gate_test.rs` (target `integration_preflight_gate`): success, failure at each of the seven stages (one table), and the single deadline. This task writes them as its RED cases at its own Step 2.

Not in this task: the Build, Seal and Publish stages of `ProductionVerifier` (Slot-09b, `142.079-T`); the four probe stages and the PA-2b real-chain cases, whose RED belongs to `142.054-T` (Slot-09c).
