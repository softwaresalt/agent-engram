title: Terminate the late-readiness monitor on terminal health outcomes without altering teardown
updated_at: 2026-08-27T18:07:44.6627376Z
---

Wire the Terminal arm into spawn_late_readiness_monitor (src/shim/mod.rs). Today the monitor loops on check_health forever until all receivers drop, so a permanently incompatible daemon is probed for the life of the session. New behavior: Ready publishes StartupOutcome::Ready and returns (unchanged); Transient continues the 50ms to 1s capped exponential backoff (unchanged); Terminal publishes StartupOutcome::Degraded { ProtocolIncompatible, reason }, emits tracing::warn!, and returns. Adding this second exit path must not disturb teardown: preserve the tokio::select! on outcome_tx.closed() VERBATIM so client-disconnect abort semantics are byte-for-byte unchanged. RECOVERY_INITIAL_BACKOFF_MS (50) and RECOVERY_MAX_BACKOFF_MS (1000) are NOT retuned.
