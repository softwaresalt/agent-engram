Migrate Startup plus legacy and v2 watcher paths to cancellation-bearing guarded permits, pre-registered empty wait loops, and one request linearization. Every permit wraps the full DB/file-capable execution. No-pending owner success passes the Released baton; retirement quiesces then acknowledges; ordinary success completes/disarms.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
1. All 109.024-T scenarios pass; Startup and both watcher drivers create and enable Notified before every final request/recheck, acquire exact permits, and preserve behavior.
2. Exact no-pending completion clears owner then invokes notify_one once after unlock. One/multiple empty waiters progress one mutex-authorized acquisition/release at a time without polling, queue/work duplication, notify-as-authority, or concurrent drivers.
3. No successor acquires/runs before exact exit acknowledgment; max one DB driver, zero old work after ack, and one ack notification call.
4. Ordinary completion disarms; cancellation/panic Drop recovers running ownership or acknowledges Retiring only after unwind. Stale finish/Drop cannot mutate a replacement.
5. Remove try-then-set and finish-then-drain; no detached mutation-capable child, forced barrier clear, mutex across await, or wire/schema/response change.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->
