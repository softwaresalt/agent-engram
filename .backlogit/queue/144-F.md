PHASE 1 DECISION OUTPUT: a MEASURED REPORT THAT RETURNS TO STAGE. 144.002-T evaluates exactly one binary threshold - HNSW construction at 50 percent or more of measured schema_bootstrap time on the reference environment with MEDIAN AND WORST CASE BOTH CONSISTENT.
