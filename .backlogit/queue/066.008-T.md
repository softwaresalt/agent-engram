Evaluate whether to vendor or generate a real tree-sitter TMDL grammar and integrate it inside the Power BI ingestion boundary, comparing against the safe line parser matured by 068-F/068-S. This is **not** blocked on any safety or constitution decision. The earlier "`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` prevents direct tree-sitter consumption" framing was a mischaracterization: Engram already consumes ten C-based tree-sitter grammar crates (eleven languages) via the fully safe `Parser::set_language(&tree_sitter_x::LANGUAGE.into())` API with zero `unsafe`, and `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` constrains only a crate's own source — a grammar binding crate encapsulates its generated FFI/`unsafe` and exposes a safe `LANGUAGE` surface (even a hand-written external `scanner.c` lives inside the grammar crate and is consumed safely), so `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` in `powerbi-tmdl-parser` still holds. Real decision axes (from the source spike, none of them safety): (1) grammar sourcing — vendor vs. generate and maintain a `grammar.js`, since no mature `tree-sitter-tmdl` crate is published; (2) an indentation-aware external scanner for TMDL's whitespace-scoped blocks; (3) grammar ABI / build fragility; (4) ROI versus the now-improved safe parser. Source spikes: docs/decisions/2026-06-12-tmdl-tree-sitter-spike.md (pivot) and docs/decisions/2026-07-04-tmdl-tree-sitter-safe-consumption-correction-spike.md (safety correction + recommendation).
