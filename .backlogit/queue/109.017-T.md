Implement atomic binding advance with one fail-closed quiescence barrier. Prepare/capacity-check the exact binding tuple, acquire binding guards in fixed order, then perform one no-await coordinator transition. A running owner becomes Retiring, not Idle: same binding moves owner mask OR pending into deferred; distinct binding moves zero old bits. Publish new binding/floor/cancellation channel, unlock, then signal old cancellation. Do not wake or admit a successor at rebind.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
1. All 109.016-T scenarios pass; capacity/overflow failures mutate nothing and new token is returned only after coherent publication.
2. Every active OwnerKind receives cancellation through its permit; the exact retired identity remains the sole barrier acknowledgment key.
3. During Retiring, current requests coalesce only in deferred and none acquires. Same-binding 0b101 OR 0b010 = 0b111 remains there; distinct binding carries zero old-workspace bits and retains reconciliation/reissue rules.
4. Exact explicit terminal or armed Drop after DB/file-capable exit moves latest deferred to ordinary pending, clears the barrier, writes no timestamp, and wakes exactly once. It returns no successor permit to the retired driver.
5. Rebind while retiring retargets the same barrier: equal target preserves deferred; distinct target discards superseded-target work. Stale terminals cannot mutate or wake.
6. No mutex crosses await and no public/wire/schema/config-format/persistence change occurs.
