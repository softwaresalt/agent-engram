Rollback or hotfix immediately if any of these occur after merge:

* respawn rate exceeds 2 per hour for the same workspace
* any confirmed stale-handle or wrong-daemon bind after `.workspace-id` creation
* any stale-PID false positive that kills or replaces a healthy daemon
* Unix fallback socket bind failures block daemon startup on affected platforms
