# Compacted Memory — 104-S / 109-F Single-Authority Coordinator

## Outcome

Feature `109-F` shipped through PR #319 as merge commit
`d8fba2c3c4538e061e2ac4f56da83f82801d78e9`. Shipment `104-S` is
archived at that SHA. The 19 replacement tasks are present in archive; feature
`109-F` remains done and archived. Superseded `109.001-T`–`109.012-T` remain
blocked and unassigned outside the shipment.

## Decisions

- One private coordinator owns binding generation, owner phase, full work
  mask, cancellation, handoff, and completion timestamp.
- Ownership moves continuously from `AdmissionGuard` to `OwnerPermit` and
  transferred permit; no receiver, permit, mask, or task handle detaches.
- Same-binding retirement preserves the complete `0b111` mask behind a
  quiescence barrier. Distinct binding carries no old work.
- Mutation-capable children end before acknowledgement, and no successor
  starts before acknowledgement.
- Hydration and Index refresh branch identity before I/O; progress is fenced
  to the exact owner.
- The coordinator/caller migration is one merge and rollback unit. Public
  CLI/MCP, wire, schema, persistence, and queued-response contracts stay
  unchanged.
