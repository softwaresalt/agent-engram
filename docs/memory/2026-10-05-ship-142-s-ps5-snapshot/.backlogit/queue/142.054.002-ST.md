Implement the Build, Seal and Publish preflight stages against the F07/F08/F10 facade, carrying the single deadline and expected generation through each.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
- Build constructs a candidate through the sealed `IndexTarget::Candidate` path only.
- Seal produces the sealed inventory and digests.
- Publish goes through the F08 atomic publication under the publisher lock.
- Each stage respects the shared deadline and fails typed on expiry.
- A failure in any stage leaves the previously published generation intact and serving.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

| Publish | `publish_sealed_candidate(.., "engram-preflight")` |
| DaemonVerified | `wait_for_generation(PREFLIGHT_READ, expected)` |
| HealthVerified | `probe_cli_read(HEALTH_READ).accepted` |
| CliProbeVerified | `probe_cli_read(PREFLIGHT_READ)` with `generation_id == expected` |
