## Scope and ownership

This contract governs the `v0.3.0-rc.1` dogfood window. The release owner
collects the signals, records a daily disposition, and decides whether to
continue, withdraw, or supersede the RC. Dogfood users supply manual reports
when existing logs and telemetry do not expose a signal.

The observation window lasts at least seven days and includes at least 20
startup sessions across Linux, Windows, and Apple ARM. Silence is not evidence
of health: the release owner records a final `healthy`, `degraded`, or
`withdrawn` disposition when the window closes.

## Rollback and withdrawal criteria

The release owner applies the first matching trigger. An immediate trigger
blocks further distribution while the owner obtains any approval required for
the withdrawal mechanics.

| Trigger | Severity | Threshold or symptom | Owner decision path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data integrity | Immediate withdraw | Any index or database corruption, irreversible workspace-state damage, or loss of user data | Stop recommending the RC, preserve diagnostics, and request approval to withdraw the release |
| Security | Immediate withdraw | Any new actionable `cargo audit` advisory that affects a shipped code path, or any credible vulnerability report against the RC | Stop distribution, assess exposure, and request approval to withdraw; do not publish remediation details before coordinated disclosure |
| Regression | High | A capability that works in `v0.2.0` is unavailable or produces incorrect results in the RC | Withdraw when data or core workflows are affected; otherwise decide within one business day whether to withdraw or supersede with `rc.2` |
| Startup and readiness | High | Fewer than 90% of expected workspaces reach Ready within 10 minutes, or any fresh or at-most-25 MB workspace misses the unchanged 30-second readiness window | Pause distribution, compare with `002-SP`, and decide within one business day whether to withdraw or supersede |
| Crash or stale state | High | Two daemon crashes for one user, three across the cohort, or any stale lock or IPC endpoint that repeatedly requires manual cleanup | Pause distribution and choose withdrawal or `rc.2` after preserving logs |
| Compatibility | Medium | MCP `initialize` or `tools/list` fails on a supported release artifact, or a supported OS archive cannot start | Stop recommending the affected artifact; withdraw the whole RC if no complete supported set remains |

The known 135 MB Cozo cold-open defect does not lower these thresholds. A
30-second readiness failure is still recorded even when the process later
reaches Ready. The measured baseline from [`002-SP`](../../.backlogit/queue/002-SP.md)
is approximately 7.5 minutes for a 135 MB database, about 1.37 GB resident
memory, and about 893 CPU-seconds.

### Withdrawal mechanics

Engram is distributed through GitHub Releases and has `publish = false`, so no
crates.io action is required.

1. Stop directing dogfood users to `v0.3.0-rc.1` and capture the trigger,
   affected platforms, and available diagnostics.
2. Obtain explicit operator approval before editing or deleting the GitHub
   Release or deleting its tag. Those actions are destructive and are outside
   the G1 preparation gate.
3. Prefer converting the RC release to a draft while preserving evidence. If
   deletion is approved instead, delete only the RC release and RC tag.
4. Publish a withdrawal notice in the next changelog entry and supersede with
   `rc.2` only after the triggering condition has a verified disposition.
5. Never delete, move, or repoint `v0.2.0`. It remains the stable **Latest**
   release throughout withdrawal and supersession.

## Dogfood observability plan

No new instrumentation is required for this RC. Use existing structured logs,
the startup-failure record documented in
[the troubleshooting guide](../troubleshooting.md#startup-failure-record),
the commands in the
[operations and diagnostics guide](../log-observation-guide.md), and explicit
manual reports.

| Signal family | Measurement | Source or query | Baseline | Alert threshold | Cadence and owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Startup and readiness success | Starts reaching Ready within the unchanged 30-second window divided by all starts; bucket by cold/warm and database-size bands `0-25`, `26-100`, `101-135`, and `>135` MB | Timestamp process start and first Ready log; correlate with `engram daemon-status`, `engram workspace-status`, and `.engram/diagnostics/shim-startup-failures.jsonl`; manual report if timestamps are unavailable | A 135 MB cold start missed 30 seconds on 2026-08-29 and reached Ready after about 7.5 minutes | Any fresh or at-most-25 MB miss; or cohort success below 90% within 10 minutes | Record every start; release owner summarizes daily |
| Cold and warm latency | Median and p95 time-to-Ready, always reported with database size in MB and cold/warm classification | Structured daemon logs plus manual stopwatch record; use the same workspace for a cold open and immediate warm reopen | Cold: about 7.5 minutes at 135 MB; no accepted warm baseline yet | Cold p95 above 10 minutes at up to 135 MB, warm p95 above 30 seconds, or any report lacking database size | Record every measured start; release owner summarizes daily |
| Crash and stale state | Daemon panics, abnormal exits, orphaned locks, and stale IPC endpoints requiring cleanup | `.engram/logs/`, process exit status, `.engram/run/`, Cozo lock state, and manual cleanup report | Zero accepted occurrences | Two crashes for one user, three across the cohort, or one repeatedly stale workspace | Review reports continuously and logs daily; release owner responds |
| Compatibility feedback | Successful MCP `initialize`, non-empty `tools/list`, clean stdin-close shutdown, client and OS/architecture | Packaged-artifact smoke record, MCP client logs, and manual dogfood report | Pre-tag workflow contract covers Linux, Windows, and Apple ARM archives; `rmcp` resolves to 1.8.0 | Any supported artifact fails the stdio contract or current Copilot cannot complete the smoke exchange | Run once per packaged artifact and collect reports continuously; release owner responds |

Every latency record uses this minimum shape:

```text
timestamp, rc_version, os_arch, database_mb, cold_or_warm,
ready_within_30s, time_to_ready_ms, exit_code, notes
```

## Stable v0.3.0 reliability acceptance gates

Stable `v0.3.0` remains blocked until [`002-SP`](../../.backlogit/queue/002-SP.md)
is resolved and all gates below pass against the unchanged 30-second readiness
window. Increasing a timeout cannot satisfy any gate.

1. **Startup success:** at least 99% of 100 cold-start trials reach Ready within
   30 seconds, with trials distributed across Linux, Windows, and Apple ARM and
   the `0-25`, `26-100`, and `101-135` MB database bands. No band may score
   below 95%.
2. **Cold latency:** cold-start median is at most 20 seconds and p95 is at most
   30 seconds for every database-size band through 135 MB.
3. **Warm latency:** warm-start median is at most 5 seconds and p95 is at most
   10 seconds for every tested band through 135 MB.
4. **Crash and stale state:** zero daemon panics, abnormal exits, orphaned
   locks, or stale IPC endpoints requiring manual cleanup during the 100-trial
   startup campaign and a seven-day dogfood window.
5. **Compatibility:** packaged Linux and Windows artifacts complete MCP
   `initialize`, return a non-empty `tools/list`, and shut down cleanly in all
   ten repeated trials per platform. The Apple ARM artifact passes the archive
   and CLI smoke gates.
6. **Evidence review:** the release owner records database sizes, cold/warm
   classification, raw time-to-Ready values, and the resolved `002-SP`
   disposition before requesting stable-release approval.

## Pre-release audit

| Check | G1 disposition |
|---|---|
| Rollout gate | RC and dogfood-only disclaimer; stable `v0.3.0` remains blocked |
| Rollback procedure | Defined above; destructive release or tag actions require separate approval |
| Data migration | None |
| Cross-service dependency | None; MCP client compatibility is observed as a dogfood signal |
| Monitoring plan | Defined above with sources, baselines, thresholds, cadence, and owner |
| Post-publish observation | Seven days and at least 20 startup sessions; release owner records the closing disposition |
